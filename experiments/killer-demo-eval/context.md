# Product context

Review only the Killer Demo's decision-readiness against Safeword's intake
standard. These are fictional, fixed product facts, not claims to research.
Other Product Plan sections have already been reviewed and are out of scope.

The audience is a small-business bookkeeper (BK). Reconciling 50 invoice
payments currently takes 45 minutes of matching bank rows to invoice records.
The product bet is reducing that work to five minutes without silently
accepting an incorrect match. The initial scope is one supported bank CSV and
one invoice ledger; ambiguous payments must remain unresolved for the user.

The prototype can compare those inputs, propose matches with source-row links,
and leave ambiguous rows for review. It cannot inspect tax returns, connect to
other banks, or certify a business's accounts. The demo dataset contains
47 unambiguous payments and three genuinely ambiguous payments. A human-checked
answer sheet exists for all 50 rows. No other validation has been performed.

The same demo standard applies whether this Product Plan belongs to a feature
epic or a standalone feature. Assess the submitted demo, without rewriting it.
