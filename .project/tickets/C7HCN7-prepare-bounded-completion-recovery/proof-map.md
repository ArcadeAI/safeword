## Certification gap

Registration validation names normally executable tests and checks outline
cardinality. A passing suite proves those assertions passed, not every semantic
Given/When/Then clause. Certification needs a case-to-assertion mapping with
parameter rows and accepted limits. Unsupported rows stay explicit. Optional
stronger proof must not become new product scope to make closure easier.

For fallback order, the mapping must cover both declaration orders and the
success/failure branches, not just cite a parameterized test title. For route
admission, distinguish matching exact identity, wrong identity and stale digests;
the degraded-labeling/empty-set test is supporting evidence, not a substitute
for those outcomes.
